❓ FAQ

Got Questions?

Most common questions answered below!

What is an eSIM?+
An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM card built into your phone. Instead of a physical card, you scan a QR code and your phone connects to a data network — no swapping SIMs needed!
Does my phone support eSIM?+
Most phones from 2018 onwards support eSIM — including iPhone XS and newer, Samsung Galaxy S20+, Google Pixel 3+, and many more. Not sure? Dial #06# — if you see an EID number, your phone supports eSIM!
How do I activate my eSIM?+
After payment, we send you a QR code + step-by-step guide on WhatsApp. Go to Settings → Mobile Data → Add eSIM → Scan QR Code. You can pre-install before your trip and activate when you land.
Can I use hotspot / share data?+
Yes! Most of our plans support mobile hotspot so you can share with travel companions. Great for families and groups travelling together!
What if I run out of data?+
Just WhatsApp us and we'll arrange a top-up plan quickly. For worry-free travel, we recommend the Unlimited plan — no tracking, no stress, just browse freely the whole trip!
How do I pay?+
Payment is done via WhatsApp — we accept DuitNow QR, online banking, and TNG e-Wallet. Simple, fast, and secure. No need to enter any card details online.
How long does it take to receive my eSIM?+
Most eSIMs are delivered within 10 minutes during operating hours (9AM–9PM). Late-night order? No worries — we'll process it within 12 hours once our team is back online.
