❓ FAQ

Got Questions?

Most common questions answered below!

What is an eSIM? +
An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM card built into your phone. Instead of a physical card, you scan a QR code and your phone connects to a data network — no swapping SIMs needed! Your existing Malaysian number stays active at the same time.
Does my phone support eSIM? +
Most phones from 2018 onwards support eSIM — including iPhone XS and newer, Samsung Galaxy S20+, Google Pixel 3+, and many more. Not sure? Dial this code on your phone to check:
📞 Dial: #06#

If you see an "EID" number in the result, your phone supports eSIM! Still not sure? Just WhatsApp us your phone model and we'll check for you 😊
How do I activate my eSIM? +
After payment, we send you a QR code + video tutorial on WhatsApp. Go to Settings → Mobile Data → Add eSIM → Scan QR Code. Done! You can pre-install it before your trip and only activate it when you land — your data validity starts from activation, not installation.
Can I use hotspot / share data? +
Yes! Most of our plans support mobile hotspot so you can share with travel companions. Great for families and groups travelling together!
What if I run out of data? +
Just WhatsApp us and we'll arrange a top-up plan quickly. For worry-free travel, we recommend the Unlimited plan — no tracking, no stress, just browse freely the whole trip!
How do I pay? +
Payment is done via WhatsApp — we accept DuitNow QR, online banking, and TNG e-Wallet. Simple, fast, and secure. No need to enter any card details online.
How long does it take to receive my eSIM? +
We aim to deliver your QR code within 10 minutes after payment confirmation during operating hours. We also send a video tutorial so setup is always easy, even for first-timers!
